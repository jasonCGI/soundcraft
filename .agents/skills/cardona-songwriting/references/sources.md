# Open-source influences

This skill contains original instructions and examples. It does not copy source-book quotations, third-party lyrics, model weights, or training data.

- [lyric-writing-skills](https://github.com/jtydhr88/lyric-writing-skills), MIT: structured lyric briefs, intent-first drafting, revision gates, and multilingual routing.
- [suno-songwriter-agent-skill](https://github.com/regiellis/suno-songwriter-agent-skill), MIT: hook-first song packages, clear separation of lyrics and production direction, personas, and album continuity.
- [VerseFlow](https://github.com/AadiCodes4/verseflow), MIT: deterministic rhyme, syllable, internal-rhyme, and flow analysis concepts.
- [rhymomatic](https://github.com/cjrh/rhymomatic), Apache-2.0: offline phonetic rhyme lookup concepts for a possible native Rust implementation.
- [syncalong](https://github.com/wickeddoc/syncalong), MIT: optional future lyric-to-audio alignment and LRC export.

The first SoundCraft analyzer is dependency-free and labels its English syllable and spelling-based rhyme results as estimates. A future phonetic backend can replace the estimator without changing the report schema.
